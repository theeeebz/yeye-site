Yeye Support

Help with routines, reminders and your subscription.

Contact

Email berti.heba@gmail.com and we'll reply within a few days. Tell us your iPhone model and what you saw.

Restore a purchase

  1. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.
  2. Open Yeye. On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchases. If you're already in the app, go to Settings → Restore purchases.

Cancel or change your plan

  1. In Yeye, open Settings → Manage subscription. You can also go to the iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Yeye.
  2. Cancel at least 24 hours before the plan renews to avoid the next charge. Yeye stays open until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders don't arrive

  1. In Yeye, open Settings and check the daily reminder is on and set to the time you want.
  2. In the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Yeye, turn on Allow Notifications.

Once you've finished today's routine, today's reminder is skipped.

No voice during a routine

  1. Check the sound button at the top of the player, and that Voice cues is on in Yeye's Settings.
  2. Turn up the volume while a routine is playing.
  3. For a more natural voice, download one in the iPhone's Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, choosing an Enhanced or Premium voice. Yeye uses it from the next launch.

Moving safely

Yeye offers general movement guidance, not medical advice. Go gently and stop if anything hurts. Each move lists when to skip it.
